A brief lab orientation

  1. Click a bottle, tap it, drag it to the mixing station, or press 1–7 to pour.
  2. Combine two or three different reagents. Listen to Gura: he notices promising pairs and unstable ones.
  3. Press Begin reaction or Enter to test your mixture.
  4. Reset with the button or R. Successful potions appear in your field journal.

A starting observation: light likes something to reflect off. Try Lumen Dew with Moonmilk.
